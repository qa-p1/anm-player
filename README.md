Allow a few minutes for the first launch; setup may ask for your password to install anything your computer is missing. The app opens in your browser. After that, open **ANM Player** from your app launcher, Start menu or Applications folder.

## 🎵 Make yourself at home

- **Search** YouTube Music, play a song, or download it for offline listening.
- **Library** keeps your albums, artists, playlists and favorites together.
- Click the **bottom player** for lyrics, your queue and playback controls.
- Press **`?`** for keyboard shortcuts.

No account or subscription. Your library lives on your machine.

## 🔄 Updates

The app checks when it opens. The button beside **power** turns into a download icon when there's an update. Click it; the app updates and restarts.

Use **power** to close the app. Closing the browser tab leaves it running.
